# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A single user managing their own software engineering and applied AI/ML career sprint.

## Product Purpose

Provide a balanced workspace for daily work and longer-term progress across a 12-week career sprint.

## Operating Context

The user plans daily work, logs actual time, reviews a weekly roadmap, tracks projects and interview practice, and manages applications.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app runs locally in the browser without an account or backend. Progress is stored in browser localStorage; JSON export and import provide backups. Roadmap goals are suggestions, not recorded achievements. Preserve existing workflows, data, light/dark/system themes, and desktop and mobile support.

## Product Principles

- Balance daily focus with long-term progress.
- Keep recorded achievements distinct from suggestions.
- Keep personal data local and portable.
