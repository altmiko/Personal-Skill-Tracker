# SkillTrack workspace

Mode: Operate. Audience: the owner of the personal career sprint. Job: balance daily execution with long-term progress while preserving all existing workflows and local data.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A learning journal connects daily work to longer-term progress through clear, usable records.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm ivory work surfaces, dark ink navigation, muted rust accents, and editorial serif headings paired with practical sans-serif controls.

**STORY:** Find today's work, see the current week and recent activity, then move between roadmap, projects, practice, and settings using familiar navigation.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Persistent navigation, date and action, progress summary, today's plan and current week. On narrow screens navigation becomes a horizontal strip.

**FORM:** Learning journal expressed through typography, palette, density, and a chronological record.

**SIGNATURE:** The seven-day record shows completed tasks by assigned date and explicitly logged hours. Each day opens in Today.
